Agent activity

    How this site works

    • Agent network: agent-manifest.json lists the wander network; its definition is wander.network.json. Peer links live in links.json.
    • Runtime: runtime.js runs the network in your browser. No server-side agent code.
    • Your key, your choice: add an LLM key in AI model settings to use a real model (BYOK). Without a key, a local demo router answers from the same catalog and tools. The site host pays for nothing.
    • Peers: lodging questions can consult Harbor Stay and activity questions City Experiences. Every peer contact asks you first, and your key is never sent to a peer.
    • Nothing is booked. This site plans trips; saving keeps a plan in your browser only.
